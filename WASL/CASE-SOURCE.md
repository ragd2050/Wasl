# Educational Case Source

## Purpose

`WS-ST-001` is a fictional, de-identified educational case created to
demonstrate the WASL workflow. It is not a patient record and does not contain
audio or personal data from the referenced publication.

## Clinical inspiration

Al-Khaledi, M., Lincoln, M., McCabe, P., & Alshatti, T. (2017).
*The Lidcombe Program: a series of case studies with Kuwaiti preschool
children who stutter*. Speech, Language and Hearing.

DOI: `10.1080/2050571X.2017.1370523`

The publication describes six Arabic-speaking Kuwaiti preschool children,
their reported stuttering features, clinical speech-sample measurement and
Lidcombe Program treatment. The WASL educational case uses only general
clinical characteristics described in the publication.

## Interpretation safeguards

- Model scores are screening outputs, not diagnoses or clinical severity.
- Percent syllables stuttered (`%SS`) must be measured by a qualified clinician.
- DLD cannot be determined from acoustic analysis alone.
- Treatment selection remains the responsibility of a qualified speech-language
  pathologist after a complete assessment.
